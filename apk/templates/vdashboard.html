🚨 Kerala Flood Relief Mission

The Kerala floods have caused widespread devastation, displacing thousands and affecting livelihoods. Heavy rains have resulted in flooding, landslides, and loss of property. Relief efforts are ongoing, but volunteers play a crucial role in ensuring aid reaches those in need.

📍 Impact:

  • Over 1 million people displaced
  • Hundreds of homes and infrastructures destroyed
  • Severe food and water shortages
  • Urgent need for medical supplies and shelter

🤝 How You Can Help:

  • Donate food, clothes, and medical supplies
  • Assist in setting up relief camps
  • Help coordinate transportation of essentials
  • Spread awareness and encourage community participation

📌 Instructions for Volunteers

  • 🔹 Always wear protective gear while working in flood-affected areas.
  • 🔹 Keep emergency contacts and first-aid kits handy.
  • 🔹 Ensure hygiene while handling and distributing food items.
  • 🔹 Coordinate with local authorities for structured relief work.
  • 🔹 Report any medical emergencies to the nearest relief center.
  • 🔹 Offer mental health support—many victims are traumatized.
